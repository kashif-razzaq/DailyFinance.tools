# Tone of voice

**Professional, direct** (per the humanize-content skill supplied by Kashif, 2026-11-06).

- Plain, measured language. Explicit actors and actions. Lead with the point.
- Every sentence must pass the reader-need test: what would the reader lose if it disappeared?
- Concrete claims only — numbers, decisions, consequences. No generic benefits, no unsupported intensifiers ("dramatically", "revolutionary"), no unnamed experts.
- No filler, no chatbot residue, no decorative formatting. Sentence-case headings.
- Distinguish facts, interpretations and speculation; place qualifications with the claims they limit.
- US English. Finance terms stay precise; explain an unfamiliar term once, briefly, when the reader needs it.
- Write for a person deciding about money: sufficiency over length, always.
